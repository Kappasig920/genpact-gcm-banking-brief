06 · Suggested call flow (25–30 min)

  • 0–3 min · Open. Thank them. State the frame: "I want to understand the seat well enough to know if a hiring-manager conversation is the right next step."
  • 3–12 min · Let them talk. Role origin, why now, which banks, success in year one, team around the seat.
  • 12–20 min · Your filters. Walk the table in §05. Be specific on remote, travel, book, and comp architecture.
  • 20–25 min · Position. 30-second frame + one proof story (quota overperformance + a governed tech transformation).
  • 25–30 min · Close. "If the book, band, and motion line up, I would like an intro to the hiring manager this week, plus the written JD and the grade/comp range."

08 · Green flags / red flags

Green
  • Named banking portfolio with executive access.
  • ATS / agentic attached to the number, not just FTE defense.
  • Recruiter can speak to grade and band on this call or within 24 hours.
  • Hiring manager is a commercial or industry leader, not only ops.
  • They want a strategist who can hold a transformation narrative.
Red
  • Vague "global customer" language with no book, no quota, no buyer.
  • Heavy voice / BPO DNA with little digital or AI attached.
  • Comp deferred indefinitely; "depends on interview feedback."
  • Relocation pressure or unexplained on-site requirement.
  • Role is backfill for a burned-out relationship with no change in offer.
